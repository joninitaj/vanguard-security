About the company

Built in Kosovo, deployed where it matters

Mbrojtje e qetë, e dukshme dhe e disiplinuar.

Vanguard Security is a privately held protection company based in Prishtina. We staff static posts, mobile patrols, and event teams with officers who know the city’s rhythm — from Mother Teresa Boulevard to industrial parks in Fushë Kosovë and hospitality corridors in Peja and Prizren.

This website is a public demonstration of our brand, services, and typical assignments. Client names on the jobs board are illustrative.

What we do

Services sized to the site, not a template

Every post gets a written standing order, radio protocol, and an escalation path to our night desk.

Static & mobile patrol

Gatehouses, warehouse loops, and vehicle patrols with timed checkpoints and incident logs.

Event security

Access lanes, bag checks, stewarding, and close-out for concerts, conferences, and private functions.

Retail & corporate

Floor presence, loading-bay control, and after-hours lock-up for offices, malls, and banks.

Residential

Compound gates, visitor logs, and overnight coverage for apartment blocks and gated communities.

VIP / close protection

Advance work, discreet escorts, and venue sweeps for visiting executives and public figures.

CCTV monitoring

Remote watch, alarm response, and coordinated dispatch with on-site officers.
